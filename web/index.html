⚙️ How NETRA Works

🎓
.EDU Spoofing Radar

Detects attackers parking fake .edu labels or university names inside subdomains to fool students.

🔤
Homoglyph Detection

Catches Cyrillic lookalikes, l→1, o→0, rn→m substitutions and Unicode confusables in domain names.

🔒
Credential Trap Scanner

Inspects DOM forms in real-time for password inputs posting off-domain, over HTTP, or harvesting NetIDs.

💸
Financial Aid Lure Detection

Catches FAFSA, tuition refund, and student bursary lures on commercial domains harvesting banking data.

🔔
Desktop Alerts

Fires deduplicated, high-priority desktop notifications on dangerous pages with smart cooldowns.

📧
Email Phishing Shield

Real-time detection on Gmail, Outlook Web, and campus webmail. Flags spoofed sender names, urgency lures, and highlights malicious email links.
