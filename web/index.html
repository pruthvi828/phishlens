How Student Shield Works (100% Local Heuristics)

🎓 .EDU Spoofing & Portal Radar

Detects attackers parking fake .edu labels or university names inside subdomains (e.g. mit.edu.portal-login.com) to fool students.

💸 Financial Aid & Tuition Scams

Catches unauthorized third-party sites using FAFSA, tuition refund, or student bursary lures to harvest banking and SSN data.

🔒 Credential-Harvest Form Traps

Inspects DOM forms in real-time for password inputs posting off-domain, over unencrypted HTTP, or targeting Student IDs / NetIDs.

🔔 Deduplicated Desktop Alerts

Dispatches high-priority desktop notifications on dangerous student sites with cooldowns that prevent notification spam.
